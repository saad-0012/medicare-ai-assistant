APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING POLICY
MediCare Health System — Outpatient Services
Version: 2.3 | Effective Date: March 2024

OVERVIEW
MediCare Health System provides outpatient appointments across multiple specialties. This policy outlines the process for booking, rescheduling, and cancelling appointments, along with departmental availability.

BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT
Patients may book appointments through the following channels:
1. Patient Portal: portal.medicare-hs.com — available 24/7 for self-service booking.
2. MediCare Mobile App: Download on iOS or Android, then navigate to "Book Appointment".
3. Phone: Call 1800-MED-APPT (1800-633-2778), available Mon–Sat, 8 AM–8 PM.
4. In-Person: Visit the outpatient registration desk during working hours.
5. Telehealth: Book virtual consultations separately through the telehealth module on the portal.

INFORMATION REQUIRED FOR BOOKING
When booking an appointment, the following information is required:
- Patient full name and date of birth
- Registered mobile number or email address
- Preferred department and doctor (if specified)
- Preferred date and time
- Brief description of the reason for visit (optional but recommended for scheduling priority)
- Insurance details (if applicable)

APPOINTMENT CONFIRMATION
- Upon successful booking, patients receive a confirmation via SMS and email.
- Confirmation includes: appointment date, time, department, doctor name, location, and preparation instructions if applicable.
- Patients are requested to arrive 15 minutes early to complete registration formalities.

RESCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT
- Appointments may be rescheduled up to 6 hours before the scheduled time without penalty.
- Rescheduling is available via the patient portal, app, or phone.
- Patients are allowed up to 3 reschedules per appointment. After 3 reschedules, a new booking must be created.
- For specialty appointments (Oncology, Cardiology, Neurology), rescheduling must be done at least 24 hours in advance.

CANCELLATION POLICY
- Appointments cancelled at least 6 hours in advance are fully refunded if a booking fee was paid.
- Cancellations made within 6 hours of the appointment may forfeit the booking fee.
- No-show appointments (patient does not arrive and does not cancel) will be marked in the patient record and may affect priority booking in the future.

PRIORITY AND EMERGENCY APPOINTMENTS
- For urgent but non-emergency medical concerns, patients may request a "Priority Appointment" by informing the booking agent of symptoms.
- Triage nurses assess the urgency and may fast-track the appointment.
- Medical emergencies must go directly to the Emergency Department — do not attempt to book an appointment for an emergency.

WALK-IN POLICY
Walk-in patients are accepted at General Medicine and Pediatrics departments on a first-come, first-served basis subject to doctor availability. Walk-in patients will be seen after all pre-booked patients for that slot. Waiting time for walk-ins may range from 30 minutes to 3 hours depending on patient load.

SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS
- Patients requiring interpreter services, wheelchair access, or other accommodations should inform the booking team at least 24 hours in advance.
- Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
- Patients above 70 years may request senior citizen priority lanes at the registration desk.

APPOINTMENT REMINDERS
- Automated reminder SMS sent 24 hours before the appointment.
- Follow-up SMS sent 2 hours before the appointment.
- Email reminder with pre-appointment checklist sent 48 hours in advance.
- Patients can opt out of reminders via Patient Portal > Notification Settings.
