INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY FAQ
MediCare Health System — Billing & Insurance Services
Version: 1.8 | Effective Date: April 2024

Q1: What insurance plans does MediCare Health System accept?
A: MediCare Health System accepts a wide range of insurance plans including:
- Government schemes: Ayushman Bharat - PM-JAY, Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS), ECHS (Ex-Servicemen), ESI (Employees' State Insurance)
- Private insurers: Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Niva Bupa (formerly Max Bupa), ICICI Lombard, Care Health Insurance, New India Assurance, United India Insurance, Oriental Insurance
- Corporate TPA (Third Party Administrator) plans tied to employer health benefits
- International travel insurance for medical tourists (select packages only)
A full list of accepted insurance providers is available at our billing desk and on the patient portal.

Q2: How do I verify my insurance eligibility before my appointment?
A: You can verify eligibility through:
1. Patient Portal: Log in > My Insurance > Check Eligibility.
2. Call the Insurance Helpdesk: 1800-MED-INSURE (1800-633-4678), available Mon–Sat, 9 AM–6 PM.
3. In-person: Visit the insurance desk at the hospital entrance at least 30 minutes before your appointment.
It is recommended to verify eligibility at least 48 hours before your scheduled appointment.

Q3: What documents do I need to present for cashless hospitalization?
A: For cashless hospitalization, bring:
- Original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID)
- Insurance policy document or e-insurance card
- TPA card (if applicable)
- Doctor's referral letter (for planned admissions)
- All previous medical records relevant to the condition
Documents must be submitted to the insurance desk at least 2 hours before admission for planned procedures.

Q4: What is the cashless hospitalization process?
A: The process is as follows:
1. Submit required documents at the insurance desk.
2. Our team sends a pre-authorization request to your insurer or TPA.
3. Insurer approves or requests additional documents (typically within 4–6 hours for planned admissions, 2 hours for emergencies).
4. Once approved, your hospitalization is covered up to the approved limit without upfront payment.
5. Any costs beyond the approved limit or excluded items must be paid out-of-pocket at discharge.

Q5: What is NOT covered under most insurance plans?
A: Common exclusions (vary by policy — always verify with your insurer):
- Pre-existing conditions during the waiting period (typically 2–4 years depending on policy)
- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures not medically necessary
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Experimental or investigational treatments
- Dental and vision care (unless specifically included)
- Maternity expenses during the initial waiting period
- Non-allopathic treatments (AYUSH) unless explicitly covered
- OPD (outpatient) expenses unless OPD rider is included

Q6: How do I file a reimbursement claim?
A: For reimbursement (when cashless was not availed):
1. Pay the hospital bill in full at the time of discharge.
2. Collect original bills, prescriptions, discharge summary, and investigation reports.
3. Submit the claim form (available at billing desk or insurer's website) along with all original documents to your insurer within 30 days of discharge.
4. MediCare's billing team can assist with claim documentation — contact billing@medicare-hs.com.

Q7: What if my claim is denied?
A: If your insurance claim is denied:
- You will receive a written denial letter with the reason.
- You may appeal the denial by submitting additional clinical documents to the insurer within 15 days.
- MediCare's patient advocacy team can assist with the appeal — contact advocacy@medicare-hs.com.
- If the appeal is also denied, you may escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman in your region.

Q8: Does MediCare accept Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)?
A: Yes. MediCare Health System is empaneled under the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme. Eligible beneficiaries can avail cashless treatment for listed medical and surgical procedures up to ₹5 lakhs per family per year. Eligibility is verified through the beneficiary's Ayushman card or mobile number-based e-card from the PM-JAY portal.

Q9: How do I get an itemized bill for insurance purposes?
A: Itemized bills can be requested:
- At the billing desk during or after discharge.
- Through the Patient Portal: Billing > Download Itemized Bill.
- Via email: billing@medicare-hs.com (processing time: 2 business days).

Q10: What is a co-payment and will I need to pay it?
A: A co-payment (co-pay) is the fixed percentage of the bill that the patient pays out-of-pocket even with insurance. Whether co-pay applies depends on your specific policy. Common co-pay scenarios:
- Senior citizen policies: often 20% co-pay.
- Network hospital discount plans: may waive co-pay.
- Government employee plans (CGHS): may have specific co-pay slabs.
Always check your policy document or ask your insurer for your co-pay terms.
