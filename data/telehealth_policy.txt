TELEHEALTH CONSULTATION GUIDELINES
MediCare Health System — Policy Document
Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: January 2024

OVERVIEW
Telehealth services allow patients to consult with licensed healthcare providers remotely via video or audio calls. MediCare Health System provides telehealth for eligible patients across general medicine, follow-up care, mental health, and chronic disease management.

ELIGIBILITY
Patients are eligible for telehealth consultations if:
- They are registered patients of MediCare Health System.
- They have a stable internet connection or mobile data connection.
- The condition does not require physical examination or in-person diagnostic tests.
- They have completed the telehealth consent form (available at the patient portal).

SCHEDULING A TELEHEALTH APPOINTMENT
1. Log in to the MediCare Patient Portal at portal.medicare-hs.com.
2. Select "Book Appointment" and choose "Telehealth / Virtual Visit".
3. Select preferred department: General Medicine, Mental Health, Dermatology, or Endocrinology.
4. Choose an available date and time slot.
5. Confirm the appointment. A confirmation email with a video link will be sent 24 hours before the session.

Patients can also call the helpline at 1800-MED-CARE (1800-633-2273) to book telehealth appointments.

MEDICATION REFILL REQUESTS VIA TELEHEALTH
Medication refill requests may be reviewed during telehealth visits under the following conditions:
- The medication is already prescribed by a MediCare physician.
- The medication does not require an in-person evaluation (e.g., controlled substances require in-person visit).
- The patient has not had significant side effects since the last prescription.
- The prescribing physician approves the refill after reviewing patient history.

Controlled substances including Schedule II medications (e.g., opioids, stimulants) cannot be refilled via telehealth and require an in-person consultation.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Device: Smartphone, tablet, or computer with camera and microphone.
- Recommended browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge (latest versions).
- Internet speed: Minimum 2 Mbps for stable video quality.
- Backup option: Audio-only call if video is unavailable.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
All telehealth sessions are encrypted using end-to-end encryption compliant with HIPAA standards. Sessions are not recorded without explicit written consent from the patient. Providers access patient records securely via the internal EHR system during the call.

CANCELLATION POLICY
- Cancellations must be made at least 4 hours before the scheduled appointment.
- No-shows without prior notice may be subject to a nominal cancellation fee.
- Rescheduling is available up to 2 times per appointment without penalty.

AVAILABLE DEPARTMENTS FOR TELEHEALTH
- General Medicine (Mon–Sat, 8 AM–8 PM)
- Mental Health & Counseling (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM)
- Dermatology (Tue, Thu, Sat, 10 AM–4 PM)
- Endocrinology (Mon, Wed, Fri, 11 AM–3 PM)
- Pediatrics (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–5 PM)
- Cardiology (Mon, Wed, 10 AM–2 PM)
- Orthopedics (Tue, Thu, 9 AM–1 PM)

LIMITATIONS OF TELEHEALTH
Telehealth is NOT appropriate for:
- Medical emergencies (call 112 or visit nearest emergency room).
- Conditions requiring physical examination, imaging, or lab tests.
- Surgical consultations or pre-operative evaluations.
- Patients who are unable to communicate verbally without assistance.
