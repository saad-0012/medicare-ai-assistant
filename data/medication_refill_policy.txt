MEDICATION REFILL POLICY
MediCare Health System — Pharmacy & Prescription Services
Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: March 2024

INTRODUCTION
This document outlines the policies and procedures for medication refills at MediCare Health System. All prescription refills must comply with federal and state regulations and must be authorized by a licensed prescriber.

GENERAL REFILL PROCEDURE
1. Patients may request refills through the Patient Portal, via the pharmacy directly, or through a telehealth consultation.
2. Refill requests should be submitted at least 5 business days before the medication runs out.
3. The prescribing physician must authorize all refills before dispensing.
4. Automated refill reminders are sent via SMS or email when a prescription has 7 days of supply remaining.

REFILL CHANNELS
a) Patient Portal: Log in at portal.medicare-hs.com > My Medications > Request Refill.
b) Pharmacy Counter: Visit any MediCare-affiliated pharmacy with your prescription ID.
c) Phone: Call the pharmacy helpline at 1800-MED-REFILL (1800-633-7345).
d) Telehealth: Request during a scheduled telehealth consultation (non-controlled substances only).
e) Mobile App: Use the MediCare mobile app under "Medications" section.

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE REFILL POLICY
Controlled substances (Schedule II, III, IV) are subject to strict regulations:
- Schedule II medications (e.g., Adderall, OxyContin, Ritalin) require a new written prescription each time. They cannot be refilled automatically or via telehealth.
- Schedule III and IV medications may be refilled up to 5 times within 6 months of original prescription date.
- Patients must present valid government-issued ID for controlled substance pick-up.
- Early refills for controlled substances will not be honored unless there is documented loss, theft (with police report), or a physician's emergency override.

CHRONIC DISEASE MEDICATION MANAGEMENT
For patients on long-term medications for chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, asthma, etc.):
- Prescriptions may be issued as 90-day supplies to reduce pharmacy visits.
- Auto-refill enrollment is available for stable, non-controlled chronic medications.
- Patients on long-term medications must have an annual review with their physician.
- Any dosage changes must be authorized by the treating physician and cannot be processed by the pharmacy independently.

INSURANCE AND COPAY INFORMATION
- Refills covered by insurance: present insurance card at the time of pick-up.
- If insurance does not cover a refill, patients will be informed of the out-of-pocket cost before dispensing.
- Generic substitutions are offered when available and clinically appropriate unless the physician has specified "Dispense As Written" (DAW).

MEDICATION INTERACTIONS AND ALLERGY ALERTS
MediCare's pharmacy system automatically checks for:
- Drug-drug interactions
- Drug-allergy conflicts
- Duplicate therapy alerts
Pharmacists will contact the prescribing physician and patient if any significant alert is triggered before dispensing.

MEDICATION EXPIRY AND DISPOSAL
- Expired medications should not be taken. Return expired medications to the pharmacy for safe disposal.
- MediCare pharmacies participate in the DEA Drug Take-Back Program for safe disposal of controlled substances.
- Do not flush medications down the toilet unless specifically instructed on the label.

EMERGENCY REFILLS
In case of emergency (e.g., travel, natural disaster), a one-time emergency refill of a 3-day supply may be provided for non-controlled chronic medications at the pharmacist's discretion.
Emergency refills are not available for controlled substances.

PRIOR AUTHORIZATION (PA)
Some medications require prior authorization from the insurance provider before dispensing:
- PA requests are initiated by MediCare's clinical team on behalf of the patient.
- PA processing typically takes 1–3 business days.
- Patients will be notified once PA is approved or denied.
- Appeals for denied PAs can be submitted with supporting clinical documentation.
